L

       User
       Bystander
       Users of the room
       The space
       Following user -->
User - respiratory difficulaties, in extreme cases silicosis
Bystander - respiratory difficulaties, in extreme cases silicosis
Users of the room - respiratory difficulaties, in extreme cases silicosis

### Control measures

<!-- Anything that's in here should also be in the syllabus somewhere, to ensure it's actually practised. -->

 - Access - No access to the tool without training.
